A one-page broadsheet summing up the world news of Thursday 26 November 2026 in a single image: 42 stories across 10 regions and 19 fields, compiled after the day ended in UTC with closing-state numbers throughout. A six-figure numbers strip sits under the masthead, followed by a UTC timeline, region chips, three illustrated leads — the US Section 301 tariff plan for 14 economies and the world's answer to it; a worldwide stock rally while Wall Street stayed shut for Thanksgiving; mines, a refinery claim and a denial in the Strait of Hormuz — ten further cards, every one with a visual or data banner, a numbered pin map, 29 briefs, and a DATE CHECK footer kept as a dated ledger. Every story carries a status tag and a named source; all illustrations are drawings made in code, no photographs. The program copies the image to /output/entry.png and verifies the copy (format, exact 1024x1024 size, byte-completeness).
